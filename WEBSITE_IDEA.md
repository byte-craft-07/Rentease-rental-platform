# RentEase - Furniture & Appliance Rental Platform

RentEase is a web-based rental platform for students and working professionals who frequently relocate for education or jobs and prefer renting essential furniture and appliances instead of purchasing them.

## Problem

Renters face:

- High upfront cost of buying furniture and appliances.
- Difficulty transporting items during relocation.
- Lack of flexible rental plans.
- Limited local rental options.
- Poor maintenance and support services.

## Primary Objectives

- Provide affordable monthly rental options.
- Offer flexible tenure plans.
- Simplify furniture and appliance access for renters.
- Improve urban living convenience.

## Secondary Objectives

- Reduce unnecessary purchases.
- Promote sustainable consumption.
- Enable easy relocation support.
- Create a scalable rental ecosystem.

## In Scope

- Responsive web platform.
- User registration and login.
- Product catalog for furniture and appliances.
- Product detail pages with rent, deposit, tenure, and availability.
- Cart and checkout.
- Delivery date and location scheduling.
- Pickup and return scheduling.
- Active rental management.
- Rental history.
- Maintenance support requests.
- Admin and vendor dashboard.
- Inventory, pricing, tenure, orders, rentals, service areas, disputes, damage claims, and reports.

## Out of Scope

- Native mobile applications.
- Cross-border rentals.
- Advanced AI-based pricing.
- Second-hand resale marketplace.

## Suggested Stack

- Stack: MERN.
- Frontend: React.js with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript.
- Backend: Node.js with Express.js REST APIs.
- Database: MongoDB with Mongoose.
- Deployment: AWS.
