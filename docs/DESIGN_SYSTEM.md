# Design System

## Selected Theme

Theme 2: Warm Lifestyle Comfort.

## Direction

RentEase should feel warm, inviting, home-focused, and trustworthy. The UI should look like a polished furniture and appliance rental marketplace rather than a generic SaaS dashboard.

Core emotional line:

> Make your temporary home feel complete.

## Palette

- Background: `#FAF6EF`
- Surface: `#FFFDF8`
- Text: `#2F241F`
- Muted text: `#76665D`
- Primary accent: `#C56A3D`
- Dark accent: `#7A3519`
- Olive support: `#7A8B5A`
- Border: `#EBDCCB`

## Hero Direction

- Headline: `Settle in faster. Rent the essentials monthly.`
- Primary CTA: `Explore Furniture`
- Secondary CTA: `See Rental Plans`
- Visual: large lifestyle image with furniture/appliance context.
- Mood: cozy, welcoming, human, and aspirational.

## UI Rules

- Use warm cream backgrounds and white product cards.
- Use lifestyle/product imagery prominently.
- Use serif display headings for emotional homepage moments.
- Use clean sans-serif text for product, cart, checkout, and dashboard information.
- Keep cards soft, rounded, and easy to scan.
- Customer pages can be warm and aspirational; admin pages should stay clean and operational while keeping the same palette.
- Avoid making the product feel like a lifestyle blog. Product rent, deposit, tenure, and availability must remain easy to scan.
- Admin screens should be denser and more operational than homepage screens.
